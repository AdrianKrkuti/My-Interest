About the New York Jets

The New York Jets are a professional American football team based in the New York metropolitan area. They compete in the National Football League (NFL) as a member club of the league's American Football Conference (AFC) East division.

The team was founded in 1959 and has won one Super Bowl championship.

Learn More

History

  • Founded in 1959 as the Titans of New York
  • Renamed to the New York Jets in 1963
  • Won Super Bowl III in 1969
  • Member of the AFC East division

Features

The New York Jets have a passionate fan base and play their home games at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. The team's colors are green and white, and their mascot is a jet plane named "Fireman Ed."
